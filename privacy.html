Triad Retail is a division of Triad Limited. This notice explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you use this website or deal with us, and what rights you have. We take your privacy seriously and process personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

01Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Triad Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 02958101). Registered office: The Stables, Holdenby House, Holdenby, Northampton, Northamptonshire NN6 8DJ.

We decide how and why personal data is collected and processed. Contact details are in section 13.

02Personal data we collect about you

Personal data means any information that can identify a living person. Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity data: first name, last name, job title, company, and technical identifiers such as your IP address and approximate location.
  • Contact data: email address, postal address and telephone numbers.
  • Correspondence: the content of emails, calls and messages you send us, including enquiries about our services.

We collect this information when you email or call us, when you give us your details at an event or meeting, when we work together on a project, and through the limited technical data your browser sends when you visit this website.

We do not collect any Special Categories of Personal Data about you (such as details of your race or ethnicity, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual orientation, political opinions, health, or genetic or biometric data), nor information about criminal convictions and offences.

We may also use aggregated data, such as statistics about how the website is used. Aggregated data is not personal data because it does not reveal your identity. If it is ever combined with your personal data so that it can identify you, we treat the combined data as personal data under this notice.

03How and why we use your personal data

We only use your personal data where the law allows us to. Most commonly we use it:

  • to respond to your enquiries and manage our relationship with you;
  • to scope, quote for, deliver and administer projects and services you have asked us for;
  • to send you information about our services where you have asked for it or where we have a legitimate business interest in doing so and you have not objected;
  • to tell you about changes to our services or to this notice;
  • to ask for feedback on our work;
  • to run our business, including accounting, billing, audit, insurance, legal and statistical purposes and systems testing;
  • to comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

The lawful bases we rely on are: performance of a contract with you (or steps taken at your request before entering one); our legitimate interests in running and promoting our business, provided your interests and rights do not override them; your consent, where we ask for it; and compliance with a legal obligation.

Where we send you marketing communications you can opt out at any time by emailing us or by using the unsubscribe link in any message.

04Who has access to your data

Within Triad Limited, your data is accessible to the staff who need it to do their jobs, including administration, client services, creative and production, finance, legal and IT.

We also use trusted third party suppliers who process data on our behalf, such as email and hosting providers, IT support companies, cloud software providers and, where relevant to a project, production and installation partners. All such suppliers are required to comply with data protection law, to act only on our instructions, and to keep your data secure.

05How long we keep your data

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes we collected it for, including to satisfy any legal, accounting or reporting requirements. In deciding how long to keep data we consider its nature and sensitivity, the potential risk of harm from unauthorised use or disclosure, whether we can achieve the purpose in another way, and the applicable legal requirements.

On request we will securely delete or return your personal data, unless we are required by law to retain it, in which case we will tell you in writing. We may anonymise personal data for research or statistical purposes, in which case we may keep and use it indefinitely without further notice to you.

06Your legal rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we collect and use your personal data;
  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure of your data where there is no good reason for us to continue processing it;
  • restrict processing in certain circumstances;
  • data portability, that is to receive certain data in a structured, machine readable format;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated decision making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We do not currently carry out any such automated decision making.

You will not normally have to pay a fee to exercise these rights. We may charge a reasonable fee, or refuse to comply, if a request is clearly unfounded, repetitive or excessive. We aim to respond to all legitimate requests within 28 days. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 13.

07Security of your personal data

We have put in place appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from being accidentally lost, used, accessed, altered or disclosed in an unauthorised way. This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Third parties who process data for us are required to maintain equivalent standards of security.

We have procedures in place to deal with any suspected personal data breach and will notify you and any applicable regulator where we are legally required to do so.

08Cookie policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. This website is designed to use as few cookies as possible and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Strictly necessary cookies

While the site is in preview and protected by a password, a single cookie named tr_access is set once you enter the correct password so that you are not asked for it again on the same device. It contains no personal data, is not shared with anyone, and expires after 30 days. This cookie is essential for the preview to work and is not set once the site is publicly launched.

Analytics and performance cookies

We do not currently use any analytics cookies. If we introduce analytics in future we will update this notice and, where required, ask for your consent first.

Third party content

The typeface used on this site is loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser requests the font files, Google may receive your IP address. Google's use of this information is governed by its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. No other third party content is embedded on this site.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's privacy and security settings. Turning off cookies may mean some features of a website do not work as intended.

09Sharing your personal data

We may share your personal data with external third parties where necessary, including government bodies, law enforcement agencies and regulators where we are required to by law, and suppliers who provide cloud, IT and business support services to us. We do not allow our suppliers to use your personal data for their own purposes; they may only process it for specified purposes and in accordance with our instructions.

We do not sell your personal data.

10International transfers

Some of our suppliers are based, or store data, outside the UK. Whenever we transfer your personal data out of the UK we ensure a similar degree of protection is afforded to it by putting in place appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or transfers to countries deemed to provide an adequate level of protection.
